I finished the LLM Engineering course by Ed Donner.

Not in 8 weeks. In 8 months. And I would not change that.

Here is the honest story.

I started in January 2025. Did three weeks of the course, then had to step away. I spent over six months deep in engineering work with my mentor, building real things, solving real problems, learning what it actually means to ship. No LLMs, no notebooks, just engineering.

In October 2025 I came back. A new version of the course had just been released, so I made the decision to start from scratch. Fresh eyes, better foundation, different person.

I was doing the course alongside my main work project at the same time. Every week stretched into weeks. Some parts I sat with for a long time before moving on. That was intentional. I did not want to run through it. I wanted to understand it.

What I built across 8 weeks of material:

Week 1-2: LLM APIs, prompt engineering, building Gradio apps, multi-model pipelines

Week 3: HuggingFace transformers, image generation with SDXL and FLUX, tokenizers, 4-bit quantization, Whisper ASR, running Llama locally

Week 4: Python to C++ and Rust code generation using frontier models, compiling and benchmarking the output, 9-model comparison

Week 5: RAG from scratch to production, ChromaDB, LangChain, embedding visualization, LLM-judge evaluation

Week 6: Amazon product price prediction, classical ML, PyTorch neural networks, OpenAI fine-tuning pipeline, 820k item dataset

Week 7: QLoRA fine-tuning of Llama 3.2-3B on 400k examples using W&B, beating GPT-5.1 on price prediction (39.85 vs 44.74 RMSE)

Week 8: Multi-agent autonomous AI system, SpecialistAgent, FrontierAgent, EnsembleAgent, ScannerAgent, push notifications, full Gradio UI

The result is not just a certificate. It is an engineering mindset. I now know what to build next, how to evaluate it, and how to think about the tradeoffs. That was the real goal.

I am continuing on the AI Engineering path. Personal projects are already in motion.

A huge thank you to Ed Donner. The course is exceptionally well designed. Rigorous, practical, honest about what matters and what does not. The kind of material that respects your time and your intelligence. Ed built something that actually changes how you think, not just what you know. If you are considering it, do it.
