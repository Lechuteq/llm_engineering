# Udemy Review — LLM Engineering by Ed Donner

**Rating: 5/5**

---

This course changed how I think about AI engineering. Not just what I know — how I think.

My story with it is unconventional. I started in January 2025, completed three weeks, then had to step away for over six months to focus on engineering work with my mentor. I came back in October 2025 to find that a new version of the course had just been released. I decided to start from scratch. It was the right call.

I did this course alongside a demanding work project. What should take 8 weeks took me 8 months. I do not regret a single extra day. I wanted to understand it, not just finish it.

Here is what I built, week by week: a web scraper and multi-provider LLM pipeline, Gradio applications, a conversational AI with SQLite tool calling, image generation with SDXL and FLUX on Google Colab, HuggingFace pipelines and raw transformer models with 4-bit quantization, a Python-to-C++ and Python-to-Rust code generator that benchmarked 9 frontier models, a full RAG system from keyword lookup to semantic chunking and LLM reranking, a product price predictor trained on 820,000 Amazon items, QLoRA fine-tuning of Llama 3.2-3B that beat GPT-5.1 on price prediction (39.85 vs 44.74 RMSE with a 3-billion-parameter model against a frontier model), and a fully autonomous multi-agent deal-hunting system with push notifications to my phone.

That last result — a fine-tuned small model outperforming GPT-5.1 — is the kind of thing that reframes your entire view of what AI engineering actually means. Ed made sure you understand why it happens, not just that it happens.

What makes this course exceptional is the design philosophy behind it. Every week builds on the last. Every abstraction is introduced at the right moment, not before. Ed does not pad the content. He does not over-explain. He trusts you to work through it. The progression from calling an API on Day 1 to deploying a multi-agent system on the cloud by Week 8 feels completely natural in hindsight.

The course also respects your intelligence. When there is something worth understanding deeply, Ed goes deep. When something is routine boilerplate, he moves past it. This is rare in technical education.

I came to this course as an engineer who understood software. I finished it as an engineer who understands AI systems. That is not a small difference.

Ed Donner put something genuinely valuable into the world with this course. If you are a software engineer who wants to build real things with LLMs and actually understand what you are doing, this is the course to take.

Thank you, Ed.

— Leslaw Nowakowski
